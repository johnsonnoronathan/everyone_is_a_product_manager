# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and remember that many people here are not engineers. Explaining something plainly is part of the job, not a favour.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab (*Report a vulnerability*, mentioning "code of conduct") or by a direct message to a maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
